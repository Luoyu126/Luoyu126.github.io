Hi there, I am Yunyi Chen (陈蕴毅) 👋

I am an undergraduate student at Xinya College, Tsinghua University, majoring in Computer Science and Technology. Currently, I am a research intern in Professor Yuan Yao's group at the Institute for AI, Tsinghua University.

## 🔭 Research & Interests

AI Research: My focus lies in LLM post-training and Computer Vision. I am fascinated by how we can make large models more efficient and aligned through sophisticated training paradigms.

## ⚽ Beyond Coding

Football: I am an active member of the Xinya College Men's Football Team.

Philosophy & Ethics: As a student of Xinya College, I spend my spare time exploring the works of Bernard Williams and Martha Nussbaum. I believe that the future of AI must be grounded in a deep understanding of human values and ethics.
